Full bio
Carine Sanadina is a Congolese-born, Jacksonville-based author, survivor advocate, healthcare professional, and recording artist whose work turns lived experience into language for healing. Her books explore hidden survival, toxic relationship awareness, faith, hope, and restoration. Her music—including Consolation, La Gentillesse, Wonderful, Womanifesto, Paranoïa Persécutive, Reason, and Halleluyah—extends that message through comfort, worship, courage, joy, and feminine strength.

Carine is available for media interviews, author conversations, healing-centered events, advocacy collaborations, and speaking engagements focused on domestic violence awareness, rebuilding self-worth, faith after adversity, immigrant experience, human-centered care, and emotional wellness.
